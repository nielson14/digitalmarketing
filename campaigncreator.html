// Panel 03 — Ad Copywriting
Write your
ad copy
This is where strategy becomes execution. Every character counts — Google's character limits are strict, and weak copy gets ignored. Write like you're competing for attention against dozens of competitors.
🔵 SEARCH AD (PPC) — Google Search Result
Appears at top of SERP
Google limits: Headline = 30 chars max · Description = 90 chars max · Display URL = 15 chars per path. Exceeding limits means Google auto-truncates your ad — your message gets cut off.
